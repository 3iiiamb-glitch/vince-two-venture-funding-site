Funding lane 01

SnapBlizz: turn issued IP into a paid market test.

The immediate objective is not a full national launch. It is a measured commercialization stage that converts existing prototypes and patent protection into supplier quotes, production samples, buyer-ready materials and a defined pilot.

Commercial starting point

Daryl retains the patent portfolio and can pursue staged commercialization, licensing by field of use, or a portfolio transaction—subject to definitive agreements.

  • Issued protection: U.S. design patents D753,375; D753,905; D754,950; and D754,951.
  • Existing evidence: physical prototypes, product photography and multiple shape concepts.
  • Stage-one proof: select one or two designs, obtain manufacturability and tooling quotes, test durability and establish unit economics.
  • Commercial milestone: move to a paid evaluation or an approximately 1,000-unit pilot only after the economics are supported.

Questions for the capital-readiness session

  1. Which lender, CDFI, SBA microloan, SSBCI or grant pathways best match each stage?
  2. Should the ventures be financed through separate entities or borrowing structures?
  3. What documentation, cash contribution, collateral or guarantee expectations should be prepared next?
  4. What is the most credible staged request: SnapBlizz first, Precision Play second, or parallel non-debt and debt lanes?
  5. Which local operator, manufacturing and patent-counsel referrals can accelerate verifiable milestones?

IP and deal guardrails
